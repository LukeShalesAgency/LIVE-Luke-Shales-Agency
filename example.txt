Acme Bike Co is open Monday through Friday 9am-6pm, and Saturday 10am-4pm. Closed Sundays and major holidays.

Acme Bike Co is located at 118 Harbor Street. Parking is available on the street and in the small lot behind the building.

Acme Bike Co sells bikes from Trek, Specialized, and our own house brand Acme Roadster. Acme Roadster bikes start at $650. Trek and Specialized models range from $800 to $3,200 depending on the model.

A standard tune-up costs $45 and takes 2-3 business days. Rush tune-up service is available for an extra $20 and can usually be done same-day if dropped off before noon.

Full repairs (brakes, gears, wheel truing, frame issues) are quoted after inspection, since pricing depends on parts needed. Bring the bike in for a free estimate.

Acme Bike Co offers custom builds — choose your frame, groupset, and components. Custom builds typically take 2-3 weeks and start at $1,200.

Bike rentals are $25/day or $90/week, and include a helmet and lock. A valid ID and credit card are required for rental.

Financing is available on bikes over $500 through a third-party financing partner, with approval usually instant in-store.

Returns are accepted within 14 days with a receipt, provided the bike is unused and in original condition. Used or ridden bikes cannot be returned but may be eligible for store credit at manager discretion.

Acme Bike Co does not currently ship bikes — all sales and pickups happen in-store at the Harbor Street location.
